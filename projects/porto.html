Unknown Porto

U8 international competition

Main

Team: Khech, Andrey Stenyushkin, White Russian Studio

Team Role: Lead Architect

Porto, Portugal

2014

Conception

The international Portuguese competition for the reconstruction of an abandoned factory in the city of Porto. According to the brief, the site was required to accommodate a gas station with public functions and a greenhouse

To preserve as much open space as possible, a modern automated parking system was chosen instead of a traditional surface parking lot. This approach allowed us to retain one-third of the site

In the freed space, an open square is formed, along which a large port crane bridge moves on rails. Its function is to transport shipping containers and assemble them into various configurations

Porto is a port city — this is evident even from its name. Using containers and a cargo crane emphasizes and expresses the character of the city.
