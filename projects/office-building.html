Headquarter Building Renovation

Renovation of the facades and surrounding area of a major oil company’s headquarters

The Soviet-era building is fully reclad with contemporary faсade systems, including aluminum panels, vertical louvers, and tinted glass

The dominant color, taken from the corporate brandbook, shapes the entire architectural expression

Bus Station and Entrance Pavilion

The project also includes new bus waiting pavilions and an entrance pavilion providing controlled access to the industrial site

Their design language follows and extends the architectural identity of the main headquarters building

Masterplan Design Code

A new masterplan was developed for the entire site. Vehicle and pedestrian flows are separated for maximum clarity and comfort of movement

Vertical façade stripes are echoed in the longitudinal patterns of the landscape design, transforming into benches or decorative linear elements across the territory

Outdoor Furniture

Custom outdoor furniture—benches and waste bins—was designed specifically for the project. They incorporate the recognizable corporate accent color and act as linear extensions of the faсade, seamlessly linking the building’s architecture with the landscape

Media Facade Night View

The vertical faсade louvers are equipped with programmable LED lighting. Located outside the residential areas, the building does not disturb nearby neighborhoods, yet creates a striking visual landmark for visitors arriving from the airport and acts as a nighttime beacon for the city
